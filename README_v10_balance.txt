Mist Earth: Survival — v10 Balance Patch

Changes:
- Rebalanced corruption start types:
  * Uncorrupted: Humanity 1600 / Mist 100 / Corruption 0 / Potential 60
  * Corrupted: Humanity 1200 / Mist 500 / Corruption 100 / Potential 45
  * Over-Corrupted: Humanity 800 / Mist 1100 / Corruption 400 / Potential 35
- No Manifestation bonus reduced from Humanity +1000 to +500.
- Aberrant Manifestation now converts 60% of current Humanity into Mist instead of all Humanity.
- Quest rewards adjusted:
  * Hard: 12,000 credits
  * Very Hard: 25,000 credits
  * Impossible: 60,000 credits / Corruption +500
- Added basic insufficient-resource guards for Skills, Abilities, Mist Gear, Equipment, Consumables, and Hideout purchases.
- Kept v9 music player behavior and Music/List path support.
